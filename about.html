Our story

Where every Smartian is treated as our own

The SMART Modern Public School is located near Dargah Hussain Abad on Shahdadkot Road, Qambar (Kamber), District Qambar Shahdadkot, Sindh. We teach children from Playgroup to Middle (Class 8) through activity-based and toy-based learning, with free computer classes for our students.

Our students proudly call themselves Smartians. Our motto is simple: Quest for Excellence.
